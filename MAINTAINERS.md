## What a maintainer does

- Reviews PRs in their area for legal accuracy and source discipline
- Runs the re-verification sweep on the cadence in
  [docs/verification-policy.md](docs/verification-policy.md)
- Triages accuracy issues, prioritising anything marked `priority:critical`
- Updates [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) when the legal position changes

## Review requirements

- Ordinary changes: **one** maintainer approval
- Changes under `skills/tax-compliance/` or `skills/employment-law/`: **two** approvals,
  because errors there have direct financial consequences for users
- Critical accuracy fixes: merged as fast as they can be checked against a primary source

## Becoming a maintainer

Contribute consistently and accurately in one area for a while, then ask. Being an
Attorney-at-Law or Chartered Accountant helps but is not required — source discipline matters
more than credentials, and a careful non-lawyer who cites the gazette is more useful than a
practitioner working from memory.

## Escalation

Code of Conduct reports go to the project lead. If the report concerns the project lead, raise
it with any other maintainer.
