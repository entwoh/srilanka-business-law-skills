# Regulators index

Who administers what, and where filings go.

## Department of the Registrar of Companies (ROC / DRC)

Everything under the Companies Act: incorporation, annual returns, director and secretary
changes, share issues, charges, beneficial ownership, winding up.

- **Portal:** https://eroc.drc.gov.lk/ — company registration and most filings
- **Forms:** https://drc.gov.lk/en/?page_id=1998
- **Fees:** https://drc.gov.lk/en/?page_id=11
- **Address:** No. 400, "Samagam Medura", D R Wijewardena Mawatha, Colombo 10
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri, 9.00am–3.30pm
- **General:** info@drc.gov.lk

Dedicated branches exist for the F15 (annual return) and F20 (director/secretary change)
filings — the highest-volume forms.

## Inland Revenue Department (IRD)

Income tax, VAT, SSCL, WHT/AIT, APIT. Taxpayer Identification Numbers (TIN).

- **Site:** https://www.ird.gov.lk/
- **Portal:** RAMIS / IRD e-Services — e-filing is mandatory for companies
- **Tax charts:** published per year of assessment; the most reliable quick reference for rates

A TIN is the practical gateway to everything else — corporate bank accounts, BOI, EPF
registration usually follow it.

## Department of Labour

EPF (employer side), gratuity, TEWA approvals, Labour Tribunals, Wages Board compliance,
workplace inspections.

- Commissioner-General of Labour, with district offices
- **TEWA approval** applications for non-disciplinary termination go to the Commissioner
- **Labour Tribunals** hear individual unjust-termination applications

## Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL)

Exchange control under the Foreign Exchange Act No. 12 of 2017; banking supervision; payment
systems; financial consumer protection rules.

- **Site:** https://www.cbsl.gov.lk/
- Foreign investment flows into Sri Lankan companies run through **Inward Investment Accounts
  (IIAs)** at licensed banks — the bank, not the CBSL, is the day-to-day gatekeeper
- **EPF is administered by CBSL** on the fund side, while the Labour Department handles
  employer compliance — a split that surprises people

## Employees' Trust Fund Board (ETFB)

ETF contributions and member benefits. Separate institution from EPF.

- **Site:** https://www.etfb.lk/

## Board of Investment (BOI)

Approval and administration of BOI enterprises, incentives, zones, and BOI-specific labour
and tax treatment.

- **Site:** https://investsrilanka.com/

## National Intellectual Property Office (NIPO)

Trade marks, patents, industrial designs registration; copyright administration.

- Under the Ministry responsible for trade
- Registration is at NIPO; enforcement is in the Commercial High Court

## Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE)

Listed companies, public offers, takeovers and mergers code, continuing disclosure.

- **SEC:** https://www.sec.gov.lk/
- **CSE:** https://www.cse.lk/

## Data Protection Authority (DPA)

Established under the PDPA No. 9 of 2022. Operational as a regulator; the substantive
obligations it will enforce are subject to phased commencement.

- **Site:** https://www.dpa.gov.lk/

## Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA)

Consumer protection, unfair trade practices, price controls, and competition matters.

## Provincial authorities

**Business Names registration is provincial**, not national. A sole proprietorship or
partnership registers with the relevant Provincial Registrar of Business Names — requirements
and fees vary by province. Local authority trade licences are also municipal.

## Which regulator for which question

| Question | Go to |
|---|---|
| Register a company | ROC (eROC) |
| Register a sole proprietorship | Provincial Registrar of Business Names |
| Issue shares to an investor | ROC (Form 6); bank/CBSL if the investor is foreign |
| Get a TIN | IRD |
| Register for VAT | IRD |
| Register employees for EPF/ETF | Labour Department (EPF) / ETFB (ETF) |
| Terminate an employee without misconduct | Commissioner of Labour (TEWA) |
| Bring in foreign equity | Licensed bank (IIA), BOI if seeking incentives |
| Register a trade mark | NIPO |
| Enforce a commercial contract | Commercial High Court (Colombo) |
