# ROC forms index

The complete list of forms published by the Department of the Registrar of Companies,
transcribed from the ROC's own download page.

**Form numbers are the single most commonly mis-stated fact in Sri Lankan company guidance.**
Secondary sources routinely invent them. This list is the authority; when in doubt, check the
ROC page directly.

## eROC-generated forms

Some forms must be **generated by the eROC system**, not downloaded and typed. As at the last
verification date:

- **Form 1** and **Form 5** — always use the eROC system-generated version.
- **Form 18** and **Form 19** — use the system-generated version when first incorporating.

Handwriting on an eROC-generated form is a common rejection reason. Scan to PDF, A4, with
signatures visible.

## Shares and capital

| Form | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Form 6** | **Notice of issue of shares** |
| Form 7 | Notice of calls on shares / particulars of obligations attached to a share |
| Form 8 | Notice of reduction of stated capital |
| Form 9 | Notice of acquisition or redemption by company of own shares |

> **Trap:** Form 6 is the share issue notice. Guidance frequently misnames this. A transfer
> of existing shares is *not* a Form 6 event — see
> [company-secretarial skill](../skills/company-secretarial/SKILL.md).

## Beneficial ownership

Introduced by the Companies (Amendment) Act No. 12 of 2025 and the Companies (Beneficial
Ownership) Regulation No. 1 of 2026. The **BO series** forms (including **Form BO 5**,
notification of the authorised person) are handled through the ROC's beneficial ownership
process rather than the general downloads page.

See [beneficial ownership reference](../skills/company-secretarial/references/beneficial-ownership.md).
