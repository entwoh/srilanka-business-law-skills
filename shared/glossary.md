# Glossary

- **Primary source**: binding legislation, judicial decision, or official instrument.
- **ROC**: Department of Registrar of Companies.
- **Registrar**: the official acting under the relevant companies-law function.
- **Beneficial owner**: a person meeting the applicable ownership or control test; verify the current definition.
- **Effective date**: date a measure legally takes effect, which may differ from publication date.
