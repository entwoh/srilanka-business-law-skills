# Glossary

Including the vernacular — how people in Sri Lanka actually refer to these things, which is
often not what the statute calls them.

## Corporate

**AoA / Articles** — Articles of Association. Sri Lanka abolished the memorandum in 2007;
the Articles are the constitutional document. A company may adopt the model Articles in the
First Schedule to the Companies Act or draft its own.

**BR / BR certificate** — "Business Registration certificate". Used loosely to mean either
the Certificate of Incorporation from the ROC (for a company) or the Business Names
registration certificate (for a sole proprietorship). Ask which one is meant — they are
completely different things.

**Beneficial owner / UBO** — the natural person who ultimately owns or controls **10% or more**
of a company, directly or indirectly, or exercises effective control. Defined by the Companies
(Amendment) Act No. 12 of 2025.

**Company Secretary** — mandatory for every company. Must hold a valid Certificate of Practice
from the ROC. Signs Form 19. A sole director cannot also be the secretary.

**eROC** — the ROC's online portal. Certificates of incorporation are now downloaded from the
eROC dashboard rather than issued physically.

**Effective control** — for beneficial ownership: control through a chain of ownership, the
ability to appoint or remove a director, or capacity to make strategic decisions.

**Form 20 branch** — ROC's dedicated counter for director/secretary change filings. People
say "I need to do a Form 20" to mean changing a director.

**PLC / (Pvt) Ltd / Ltd** — Public Limited Company (listed) / Private Limited Company /
limited company. Private companies are capped at 50 shareholders.

**Solvency test** — the statutory test a company must satisfy before a distribution, buy-back
or reduction of capital: able to pay debts as they fall due, and assets exceed liabilities.

**Stated capital** — replaces the older "authorised/paid-up capital" concept. Sri Lankan
companies have no authorised capital ceiling.

## Tax

**APIT** — Advance Personal Income Tax. Employer withholding on employment income. Replaced PAYE.

**AIT** — Advance Income Tax. Withholding on interest and certain other payments.

**RAMIS** — the IRD's online system. E-filing is mandatory for companies.

**SSCL** — Social Security Contribution Levy. A turnover-based levy, separate from VAT.

**SVAT** — Simplified VAT scheme. Long slated for abolition; check current status.

**TIN** — Taxpayer Identification Number, from the IRD.

**Y/A** — Year of Assessment, running **1 April to 31 March**. Not the calendar year.
Getting this wrong throws off every deadline.

## Employment

**EPF** — Employees' Provident Fund. 12% employer + 8% employee. Employee-facing retirement fund.

**ETF** — Employees' Trust Fund. 3%, **employer only** — never deducted from the employee.

**Gratuity** — statutory end-of-service payment. Employers of 15+ workmen, employees with 5+
completed years. Half a month's terminal salary per completed year (monthly-rated staff).

**TEWA** — Termination of Employment of Workmen (Special Provisions) Act No. 45 of 1971.
Requires Commissioner of Labour approval (or employee consent) for non-disciplinary termination
where the employer has 15+ employees.

**Labour Tribunal** — where an individual employee brings an unjust termination claim. Broad
jurisdiction to make a "just and equitable" order.

**Workman** — the statutory term. Broader than "manual worker"; scope matters for TEWA and
gratuity.

**Poya** — monthly full-moon Buddhist public holiday. Sri Lanka has an unusually high number
of public holidays; budget for it.

## Foreign investment

**BOI** — Board of Investment. "A BOI company" means an enterprise with a BOI agreement,
carrying distinct tax, customs and sometimes labour treatment.

**IIA** — Inward Investment Account. The designated account through which foreign investment
must flow if the investor wants to repatriate capital and dividends freely later.

**Port City** — Colombo Port City Economic Commission zone. A separate legal regime: its own
tax, exchange control and employment rules. Do not assume mainland rules apply.

## General

**Attorney-at-Law** — Sri Lanka has a fused profession: no separate solicitors and barristers.

**Commercial High Court** — the High Court of the Western Province (Colombo) exercising
commercial jurisdiction. Where significant commercial disputes go.

**Gazette / Extraordinary Gazette** — the official publication. Commencement of an Act is
typically by Order published in an Extraordinary Gazette. Cited by number/sub-number and date,
e.g. *Gazette No. 2480/48 of 21 March 2026*.

**Notary Public** — in Sri Lanka, a distinct professional office with statutory functions,
especially for land. Not the light-touch notarisation of some other jurisdictions.

**Roman-Dutch law** — the residual common law of Sri Lanka, governing contract and obligations
where no statute applies. Sri Lanka is a mixed jurisdiction: Roman-Dutch base, English
commercial law overlay, statutory modification.

**Thesawalamai / Kandyan / Muslim law** — personal laws applying to particular communities,
mainly in family and property matters. Can affect who may deal with property and is out of
scope for this project.
