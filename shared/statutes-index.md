## Data protection

| Statute | Subject | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022 | Data protection framework, Data Protection Authority | ⚠️ **Partially in force — see below** |
| Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025 | Removed the fixed grace periods for operationalisation | In force |

> ⚠️ **PDPA commencement is contested and must be verified before relying on it.**
> Parts VI, VIII, IX and X commenced 1 December 2023. A gazette notified 18 March 2025 for
> Parts I, II, III and VII. The Amendment Act No. 22 of 2025 then removed the fixed grace
> periods, and at least one credible source reports that as at mid-2026 no substantive
> commencement order had been published for the core obligations. **Check the Data Protection
> Authority (dpa.gov.lk) and the current gazette position directly.**
