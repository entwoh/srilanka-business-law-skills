# EPF, ETF and gratuity

Three separate obligations. They are routinely confused with each other.

## Summary

| | **EPF** | **ETF** | **Gratuity** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Statute | EPF Act No. 15 of 1958 | ETF Act No. 46 of 1980 | Payment of Gratuity Act No. 12 of 1983 |
| Employer pays | **12%** of total monthly earnings | **3%** of total monthly earnings | Lump sum on termination |
| Employee pays | **8%** of total monthly earnings | **Nothing** | Nothing |
| Frequency | Monthly | Monthly | Once, at end of service |
| Applies to | All employers | All employers | Employers of **15+** workmen |
| Qualifying service | From day one | From day one | **5 completed years** |

**Total employer social cost: 15% of earnings** (12% EPF + 3% ETF), uncapped, on top of salary.

## EPF

Established to provide superannuation benefits to private and corporate sector employees
through a contributory mechanism, so an employee has a lump sum in old age.

- Employer: minimum **12%** of total monthly earnings
- Employee: minimum **8%**, deducted at source
- The employer remits both together
- The employee sees **one** EPF line on the payslip, not two

"Total monthly earnings" is broader than basic salary. Confirm what is included for your
payroll — allowances are commonly caught, and getting the base wrong produces a systematic
shortfall across every employee.

**Registration is from the first day of employment.** There is no probationary carve-out and
no minimum period before contributions start.

Late contributions attract **surcharges**, which accumulate.

## ETF

- Employer: **3%** of total monthly earnings
- Employee: nothing
- **ETF is employer-only. It must never be deducted from the employee's pay.** Doing so is
  unlawful and one of the more common payroll errors in small Sri Lankan businesses.

Administered by the Employees' Trust Fund Board, a separate institution from EPF.

## Gratuity

A lump sum in recognition of service, **over and above** EPF and ETF.

### When it's payable

- The employer has employed **more than 15 workmen** in any industry during the **twelve months
  preceding the termination** of the workman in question, **and**
- the workman has completed **5 years of service** with that employer.

Note the reference period: it looks back over the preceding twelve months, not at headcount on
the termination date. An employer who has shrunk below 15 may still be caught.

### How much

| Employee type | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Monthly paid** | Half of the monthly terminal salary × each completed year of service |
| **Daily paid** | 14 days' salary × each completed year (at the terminal daily rate) |
| **Piece rated** | 14 days' salary earned at piece rate × each completed year |

Worked example — monthly-rated employee, terminal salary LKR 200,000, 8 completed years:

> 200,000 × 0.5 = **LKR 100,000 per year**
> 100,000 × 8 = **LKR 800,000 gratuity**

### Timing

Statutory deadlines apply for payment after termination, with **surcharges for late payment**.
Verify the current deadline and surcharge rate before advising.

### Payable on any termination

Gratuity is generally due whether the employee resigns, is dismissed, retires or dies (subject
to the statutory exceptions — verify the position on dismissal for specified misconduct).
It is **not** a reward for leaving on good terms.

## Budgeting reality

For an employer of 15 or more, the true cost of an employee is not the salary. It is:

- salary
- **+15%** EPF and ETF, monthly
- **+ accruing gratuity** at roughly half a month per year after year five
- **+ potential termination compensation** (capped at LKR 2,500,000 per workman where the
  Commissioner orders it)
- **+ Labour Tribunal exposure**

The monthly contributions are predictable and flat at any salary level, which makes budgeting
straightforward. The end-of-relationship costs are the ones that surprise people, and they
should be provided for from day one rather than met from cash flow when someone leaves.

## Compliance checklist

- [ ] Registered as an employer for EPF (Labour Department) and ETF (ETF Board)
- [ ] Every employee enrolled from their first day, including probationers
- [ ] Contribution base correctly identified (not just basic salary)
- [ ] EPF 12% + 8% and ETF 3% remitted monthly, on time
- [ ] ETF **not** deducted from employees
- [ ] Gratuity provision accruing in the accounts for employees approaching 5 years
- [ ] Headcount monitored against the 15-employee threshold over rolling 12 months
- [ ] Anyone engaged as a "consultant" tested against the employment indicators
