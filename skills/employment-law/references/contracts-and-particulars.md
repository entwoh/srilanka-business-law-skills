# Employment contracts, particulars and classification

## Written particulars are mandatory

Employers must give employees **written particulars of employment**. This is not optional and
not satisfied by an offer email alone.

Include at minimum:

- Names of employer and employee, and date employment began
- Job title and duties
- Place of work
- Remuneration: amount, components, payment frequency and method
- Working hours and rest days
- Leave entitlements
- Notice period
- Probation period and confirmation process
- Disciplinary and grievance procedure
- Confidentiality and IP assignment
- Any restrictive covenants

Sri Lankan employment relationships that go wrong usually turn on what the parties can prove
was agreed. Documentation is the cheapest risk control available.

## Probation

Probation is common and enforceable, but understand its limits:

- **EPF and ETF apply from day one.** There is no probationary exemption.
- Service during probation **counts** toward continuous service for gratuity purposes.
- Confirmation should be in writing. Letting probation lapse silently generally means the
  employee is confirmed.
- Termination during probation is easier in practice but does not put the employer outside the
  Labour Tribunal's jurisdiction.

## Fixed-term contracts

Use for genuinely finite work — a defined project, a fixed-duration cover.

Repeated renewals of a fixed-term contract for what is really ongoing work can create an
expectation of continued employment, and a tribunal can look at substance over form. If the
role is permanent, employ the person permanently.

## Employee or independent contractor

This is the highest-frequency classification risk in Sri Lanka, particularly in IT, creative
services and consulting, where "we'll put you on a contractor agreement" is routine.

**Courts examine the substance**, not the label. The indicators include:

| Points toward employment | Points toward contractor |
|---|---|
| Employer controls how work is done | Contractor decides method |
| Economic dependence on one payer | Multiple clients |
| Integrated into the business | Operates separately |
| Employer provides tools and workspace | Provides own tools |
| Regular fixed payments | Invoices per project |
| Cannot profit from efficiency | Can profit from efficient management |
| Cannot send a substitute | Can subcontract |

### What misclassification costs

If a "consultant" is found to be an employee:

- **Back EPF and ETF** for the whole period, plus surcharges
- **Gratuity**, if 5+ years and the employer has 15+ staff
- Accrued leave
- **TEWA protection** — meaning the termination that prompted the claim may itself have been
  unlawful
- Labour Tribunal jurisdiction
- Tax consequences from the recharacterisation

The exposure accumulates silently for years and crystallises when the relationship ends badly
— which is exactly when the person has a reason to bring a claim.

**Practical test:** if you would be upset if they took another client tomorrow, worked their
own hours, and sent a substitute — they are probably an employee. Employ them properly.

## Restrictive covenants

Non-compete and non-solicit clauses are enforceable only so far as they are **reasonable** in
scope, duration and geography, and protect a legitimate business interest. A blanket
prohibition on working in the industry will not survive.

Narrow and specific beats broad and aspirational. A twelve-month non-solicit of named clients
is far more likely to be enforced than a two-year national non-compete.

## IP assignment

Include an express assignment of IP created in the course of employment. Do not rely on
default rules to produce the result you want, particularly for contractors — for a
contractor, IP ownership should be dealt with expressly in the contract or it may not vest in
the company at all. See [`../../intellectual-property/SKILL.md`](../../intellectual-property/SKILL.md).

## Hiring checklist

- [ ] Written particulars issued before or on day one
- [ ] EPF and ETF registration from day one
- [ ] Probation terms and confirmation process in writing
- [ ] Correct classification — employee, not "consultant", if the indicators point that way
- [ ] APIT set up if earnings exceed the relief threshold
- [ ] Leave and attendance recording that satisfies the applicable statute
- [ ] Confidentiality and IP assignment included
- [ ] Restrictive covenants narrow enough to be enforceable
