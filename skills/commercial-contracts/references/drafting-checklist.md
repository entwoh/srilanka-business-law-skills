# Drafting checklist

## Parties and authority

- [ ] Full legal names, company registration numbers, registered addresses
- [ ] For a company: confirm the signatory's authority against the Articles and the ROC record
- [ ] Board resolution obtained where required
- [ ] Any shareholders' agreement consent obtained

## Commercial core

- [ ] **Scope** — what exactly is being supplied, in what quantity, to what specification
- [ ] **Price** — amount, currency, taxes (state whether VAT-inclusive or exclusive; ambiguity
      here causes more disputes than almost anything else)
- [ ] **Payment terms** — timing, method, late payment interest
- [ ] **Withholding** — who bears WHT, and whether payment is grossed up. For payments to
      individuals and non-residents, see [`../../tax-compliance/references/withholding.md`](../../tax-compliance/references/withholding.md)
- [ ] **Delivery / performance** — timing, place, acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Term and renewal**

## Risk allocation

- [ ] **Warranties** — what each party promises is true
- [ ] **Indemnities** — separate from warranties; specify the trigger and the scope
- [ ] **Limitation of liability** — cap, and exclusion of indirect and consequential loss.
      Note that exclusions of liability for fraud and certain statutory liabilities will not hold
- [ ] **Insurance** requirements
- [ ] **Force majeure** — define the events and the consequences. Do not assume a Sri Lankan
      court will imply a generous doctrine; draft it

## Termination

- [ ] Termination for convenience — notice period, who has it
- [ ] Termination for breach — material breach, cure period
- [ ] Termination for insolvency
- [ ] Consequences: transition assistance, return of materials, survival of clauses

## Confidentiality and IP

- [ ] Definition of confidential information and the exclusions
- [ ] Duration of the obligation
- [ ] **IP ownership** — who owns pre-existing IP, who owns what is created. For contractors,
      IP will not necessarily vest in the customer by default. Deal with it expressly.
- [ ] Licence grants: scope, exclusivity, territory, duration, sublicensing

## Restrictive covenants

Non-compete and non-solicit clauses are enforceable only if **reasonable** in scope, duration
and geography, protecting a legitimate interest. Narrow and specific beats broad. A blanket
industry prohibition will not survive.

## Data

If personal data is being shared or processed, address it — controller/processor roles,
security, breach notification, and any cross-border transfer. See
[`../../data-protection/SKILL.md`](../../data-protection/SKILL.md).

## Currency and cross-border

- [ ] **Currency of payment** — where a foreign currency is used, confirm the exchange control
      position under the Foreign Exchange Act No. 12 of 2017
- [ ] Exchange rate mechanism and who bears currency risk
- [ ] For inbound investment, routing through an Inward Investment Account. See
      [`../../foreign-investment/references/exchange-control.md`](../../foreign-investment/references/exchange-control.md)

## Boilerplate that actually matters

- [ ] **Governing law** — Sri Lankan law, unless there is a real reason otherwise
- [ ] **Dispute resolution** — see below
- [ ] **Notices** — addresses, method, when deemed received. Include email, and make it work
- [ ] **Entire agreement**
- [ ] **Variation** — in writing, signed
- [ ] **Assignment and subcontracting**
- [ ] **Severability**
- [ ] **Counterparts and electronic execution**
- [ ] **Language** — if there are translated versions, state which prevails

## Dispute resolution

For commercial contracts of any size, **arbitration under the Arbitration Act No. 11 of 1995**
is usually preferable to litigation: it is faster than the courts, private, and awards are
enforceable internationally under the New York Convention.

If arbitration, specify: seat, rules, number of arbitrators, language, and appointing authority.
An incomplete arbitration clause produces a preliminary dispute about the dispute mechanism,
which is the worst of both worlds.

If litigation, the **Commercial High Court** in Colombo has commercial jurisdiction.

See [`../../dispute-resolution/SKILL.md`](../../dispute-resolution/SKILL.md).

## Consumer contracts

If you are contracting with consumers, the **Consumer Affairs Authority Act No. 9 of 2003**
imposes requirements and restricts unfair practices. Standard-form terms that would be
acceptable B2B may not be.

## Before signing

- [ ] Stamp duty position confirmed
- [ ] Signatory authority verified
- [ ] Execution formalities correct
- [ ] Both parties keep an executed original
