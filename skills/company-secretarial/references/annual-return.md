# Annual return (Form 15)

## Which form

| Company type | Form |
|---|---|
| Company with share capital (the normal case) | **Form 15** |
| Company limited by guarantee | **Form 15A** |

The ROC operates a dedicated **F15 branch** — it is one of the two highest-volume filings.

## When it's due

- **First annual return: within 18 months of incorporation.** Not twelve. Not the first
  anniversary. This is the single most commonly missed company deadline in Sri Lanka.
- **Thereafter: annually.**

Confirm the exact anniversary basis for subsequent years against the Act and the ROC's own
guidance — practice on the reference date has varied.

## What it contains

The annual return is a snapshot of the company's position:

- Registered office address
- Directors and secretary, with particulars
- Shareholders and their holdings
- Stated capital
- Location of registers and records

Because it's a snapshot, **inconsistencies with your other filings will show up**. If a
director resigned and no Form 20 was filed, the annual return either repeats the error or
contradicts the register. Reconcile before filing.

## Late filing

Reported penalties: up to **LKR 100,000 for the company and LKR 50,000 per director**.

Note the structure — directors are fined **personally and separately** from the company. A
dormant company with a director who has forgotten about it still generates personal liability.

> ⚠️ Verify current penalty levels with the ROC. Penalty figures in secondary guidance drift.

Persistent default can also lead the Registrar toward strike-off action.

## The Annual Company Registration Levy

Separate from the annual return, and separately missed. The ROC publishes an application form
for the levy. Pay it annually.

## Practical sequence

1. Reconcile the register of shareholders against every share issue and transfer during the year
2. Reconcile the register of directors against every Form 20 filed
3. Confirm the registered office matches the last Form 13
4. Confirm stated capital reflects every Form 6, Form 8 and Form 9 filed
5. Confirm the beneficial ownership position is current
6. Have the Company Secretary prepare and file Form 15
7. Pay the annual levy

## Dormant and non-trading companies

There is no filing holiday for a company that isn't trading. If a company is genuinely finished
with, wind it up or apply for removal rather than leaving it to accumulate default penalties
against its directors. See [`../../dispute-resolution/references/insolvency.md`](../../dispute-resolution/references/insolvency.md).

## If you're already late

1. File as soon as possible — exposure compounds with time
2. Work out how many years are outstanding; each is a separate default
3. Bring the related registers and forms up to date in the same exercise
4. Take advice on penalty mitigation through your Company Secretary
