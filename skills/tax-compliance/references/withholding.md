# Withholding tax, AIT and APIT

## The three things people conflate

| | What it is | On what |
|---|---|---|
| **WHT** | Withholding tax | Certain payments — services, dividends, rent, royalties |
| **AIT** | Advance Income Tax | Interest and certain other payments |
| **APIT** | Advance Personal Income Tax | Employment income — replaced PAYE |

All three are collected at source and credited against the recipient's final liability.

## APIT — employment income

Employers deduct APIT monthly from employee remuneration, including salaries, bonuses and
non-cash benefits.

| | Amount | Effective |
|---|---|---|
| Tax-free threshold | **LKR 150,000 per month** | 1 April 2025 |
| Annual personal relief | **LKR 1,800,000** | 1 April 2025 |
| Rate band | Progressive, **6% to 36%** | |

The relief was increased to LKR 1.8m for resident individuals and for non-resident individuals
who are citizens of Sri Lanka, applicable from Y/A 2025/2026 (IRD Notice PN/IT/2025-01).

Note the ordering in payroll: the employee's 8% EPF contribution comes off first, then income
tax is computed on what remains against the personal relief.

**Verify the current band thresholds.** The Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 2 of 2025
restructured them; the published schedule steps through bands at LKR 1,000,000 intervals
reaching 36% above LKR 2,500,000 of taxable income. Confirm against the IRD chart for the
relevant Y/A.

## WHT on services to independent service providers

At present, a service fee exceeding **LKR 100,000 per month** with a source in Sri Lanka, paid
to a **resident individual who is not an employee** of the payer, is subject to WHT at **5%**.

"Independent service providers" has been limited to: doctor, engineer, accountant, lawyer,
software developer, researcher, academic, or any individual service provider prescribed by
regulation.

The **Inland Revenue (Amendment) Bill published 24 February 2026** proposed **expanding** this
list to include auditors, modellers, personal trainers, coaches, valuers, artists, actors,
dancers, singers, musicians and others.

> ⚠️ **Verify whether that Bill was enacted and commenced.** If it was, a much wider range of
> payments to individuals now requires withholding, and payers who miss it become liable.

This is the WHT rule most commonly missed by small businesses paying freelancers.

## Non-residents and treaties

Payments to non-residents may attract withholding at domestic rates, reduced by an applicable
double taxation agreement. Treaty relief usually requires a tax residency certificate and
sometimes advance clearance — build the lead time into the payment schedule.

Sri Lanka has a reasonably wide treaty network. Check the specific treaty rather than assuming
a standard rate.

## The payer's exposure

Withholding obligations fall on the **payer**. If you fail to withhold, you are generally
liable for the tax you should have withheld, plus penalties — and recovering it from the
recipient afterwards is a commercial problem, not a legal remedy.

For any recurring payment, establish at the outset whether withholding applies. For a
one-off, check before paying rather than after.
