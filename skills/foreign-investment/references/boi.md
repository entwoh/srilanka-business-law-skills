# Board of Investment (BOI) status

## What BOI status is

The **Board of Investment of Sri Lanka Law, No. 4 of 1978** allows the BOI to enter into
agreements with enterprises, granting concessions in exchange for investment and employment
commitments.

**BOI status is layered on top of an entity.** You still incorporate a company with the ROC;
the BOI agreement is separate and additional. "A BOI company" means a company with a BOI
agreement, not a distinct legal form.

## What it can provide

Depending on the agreement and the sector:

- Tax concessions, including enhanced capital allowances
- Customs and import duty treatment on capital goods and inputs
- Facilitation of approvals and permits
- In some zones, distinct labour arrangements
- Land or zone facilities

**Verify what is currently available.** Sri Lanka's incentive regime was substantially reformed
during the post-2022 IMF programme, and tax holidays that older guidance describes may no
longer exist. In particular, the removal of the service export exemption from 1 April 2025
changed the calculus for IT and BPO enterprises significantly.

## What it costs you

- **Minimum investment thresholds** — verify the current figures by sector
- **Employment commitments**
- **Export requirements** in some categories
- Ongoing **reporting to the BOI**
- Conditions in the agreement itself, which bind the enterprise

## Is it worth pursuing?

**Likely worth it:**
- Export-oriented manufacturing
- Large capital investment
- Enterprises in currently promoted sectors
- Businesses importing significant capital equipment
- Anything needing zone facilities

**Likely not worth it:**
- Small domestic services businesses
- Businesses below the investment threshold
- Anything where the compliance overhead exceeds the concession value

Model the actual concession against the actual compliance cost before applying. The
administrative load is real, and for a small enterprise it can exceed the benefit.

## Process, in outline

1. Preliminary discussion with the BOI on eligibility and sector fit
2. Formal application with a project proposal — investment, employment, timeline, exports
3. Evaluation and negotiation
4. **BOI agreement executed**, setting out concessions and obligations
5. Implementation against the agreed timeline
6. Ongoing reporting and compliance

Timelines vary considerably. Do not build a financing timetable around an assumed approval date.

## Interaction with everything else

- **Tax:** the BOI agreement may modify the position in
  [`../../tax-compliance/SKILL.md`](../../tax-compliance/SKILL.md). Read the agreement, not
  just the Act.
- **Employment:** some BOI arrangements have distinct terms. Check the agreement before
  advising on termination or benefits —
  [`../../employment-law/SKILL.md`](../../employment-law/SKILL.md).
- **Exchange control:** BOI enterprises may have particular treatment. Verify.
- **Company law:** unchanged. The Companies Act, annual returns, beneficial ownership and all
  ROC filings apply exactly as they do to any other company.
