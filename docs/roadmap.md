# Roadmap

Priorities are set by what causes real harm when it's wrong or missing.

## Now — get the foundation right

- [ ] Verify every `needs-verification` file against primary sources
- [ ] Complete `shared/statutes-index.md` with amendment history for each entry
- [ ] Beneficial ownership coverage fully verified against Gazette No. 2480/48 and ss 130A–130J
- [ ] Tax files re-checked against the current IRD tax chart
- [ ] `scripts/validate_frontmatter.py` and `check_staleness.py` wired into CI
- [ ] Worked examples for the three most common transactions: incorporation, share issue to a
      new investor, termination with Commissioner approval

## Next — depth where it's needed

- [ ] **Sinhala and Tamil translations** of the highest-traffic reference files. This is the
      biggest accessibility gap in Sri Lankan legal information and the most valuable thing
      contributors could do.
- [ ] Document templates — board resolutions, share transfer forms, employment contract
      skeletons — clearly marked as drafts requiring review
- [ ] Case law layer: leading Supreme Court and Court of Appeal authorities on directors'
      duties, oppression, and wrongful termination
- [ ] Colombo Port City special regime (distinct tax, labour and exchange control treatment)
- [ ] Sector licensing: banking, finance companies, insurance, telecoms, education, healthcare

## Later

- [ ] Partnerships and sole proprietorships — Business Names registration is provincial, so
      this needs nine provincial variations
- [ ] Insolvency and winding up in practical detail
- [ ] Public listing and continuing obligations under SEC and CSE rules
- [ ] Mergers, amalgamations and schemes of arrangement
- [ ] Competition and consumer protection under the CAA Act
- [ ] Environmental licensing (CEA) for manufacturing
- [ ] A structured machine-readable layer (`statutes.yaml`) so rates and deadlines can be
      queried rather than parsed from prose

## Explicitly out of scope

- Criminal law
- Family, succession and personal laws (Kandyan, Thesawalamai, Muslim law)
- Land transactions — too high-stakes and too notarially specific for a general knowledge base
- Constitutional and administrative law
- Anything presented as a substitute for an Attorney-at-Law

## How to influence this

Open an issue. Real problems people are hitting beat theoretical completeness every time —
if three people ask about the same thing, it moves to the top.
