# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be useful to a solo founder in Matara as much as to a corporate
department in Colombo. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, seniority, ethnicity, religion, language, gender, disability, or
whether you are a lawyer at all.

## Expected behaviour

- **Argue from sources.** "Section 58 says X" is an argument. "I've been practising twenty
  years" is not, on its own.
- **Assume good faith.** Most wrong content here is honestly wrong, not carelessly wrong.
- **Correct without contempt.** Someone reporting an error is doing the project a favour,
  however bluntly they put it.
- **Respect the language barrier.** Contributors write in English, Sinhala and Tamil at
  varying levels of fluency. Engage with the point, not the grammar.
- **Accept correction.** Including maintainers. Especially maintainers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind
- Communal, ethnic or religious hostility — this project covers a country where that
  carries real weight, and there is no version of it we will tolerate
- Publishing others' private information
- Using the repository to solicit clients, advertise services, or drive referral traffic
- Deliberately introducing inaccurate legal content
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing
the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers at the address in `MAINTAINERS.md` (or by opening a confidential
issue if the platform supports it). Reports are handled privately.

Responses escalate: private warning → temporary ban → permanent ban. Deliberate introduction
of false legal content is a permanent ban on the first instance, because people may act on it.
