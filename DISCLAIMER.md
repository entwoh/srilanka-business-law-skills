# Disclaimer

## This is not legal advice

Nothing in this repository is legal advice, and using it does not create an
attorney-client relationship with any contributor.

Under the Judicature Act and the rules governing the legal profession in Sri Lanka, advising
on the law, drafting instruments for others as a business, and appearing before courts and
tribunals are reserved to Attorneys-at-Law. Certain filings additionally require a licensed
Company Secretary or a Chartered Accountant. This project does not substitute for any of them.

## What it is

A structured, cited research aid. It is designed to help you:

- understand the shape of an obligation before you meet your advisor,
- know which statute, section, form and regulator applies,
- prepare a first draft, checklist or question list,
- spot an issue you would otherwise have missed.

It is **not** designed to help you decide a contested question, run a transaction unadvised,
or determine your own tax position.

## Accuracy limits you should assume

1. **It may be out of date.** Sri Lankan tax rates, thresholds and labour figures change
   annually, often mid-year, and sometimes retrospectively. Check `last_verified`.
2. **Commencement is a trap.** An Act certified by the Speaker may not be in force. Parts of
   an Act may be in force while others are not (the PDPA is the standing example).
3. **Sources conflict.** Reputable professional firms publish inconsistent accounts of the
   same amendment. Where we know of a conflict we flag it, but we won't always know.
4. **Practice differs from text.** What the Registrar, the IRD or the Department of Labour
   actually accepts may differ from what the statute appears to require.
5. **Automated summaries can be wrong.** Any tool built on this content can still misread,
   over-generalise, or state something incorrectly. Verify anything you act on.

## Specifically, do not rely on this for

- Determining a final tax liability or filing position
- Deciding whether a termination is lawful
- Litigation strategy, limitation periods, or anything with a deadline
- Regulatory licensing (banking, insurance, securities, telecoms, pharmaceuticals)
- Criminal exposure of any kind
- Anything involving land
