# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are recorded here.

Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Because this is a legal knowledge base rather than software, entries emphasise **what the
law says now** and **when the change took effect**, not just when we wrote it down.

Each entry uses one of these markers:

- `LAW` — the underlying law changed
- `FIX` — our content was wrong and has been corrected
- `ADD` — new coverage
- `VERIFY` — existing content re-checked against primary sources, no substantive change

## [Unreleased]

### ADD
- Initial ten skills, shared indexes, contributor documentation.

### LAW
- **Beneficial ownership regime in force 30 March 2026.** Companies (Amendment) Act No. 12
  of 2025 inserted ss 130A–130J into the Companies Act No. 7 of 2007; Companies (Beneficial
  Ownership) Regulation No. 1 of 2026 (Extraordinary Gazette No. 2480/48 of 21 March 2026).
  Companies existing at the operative date have a transitional window to file. Covered in
  `skills/company-secretarial/references/beneficial-ownership.md`.
- **VAT on non-resident digital services from 1 July 2026**, and financial services VAT rate
  to 20.5% from the same date. VAT (Amendment) Act No. 14 of 2026.
- **National minimum wage LKR 30,000/month (LKR 1,200/day) from 1 January 2026.**

### FIX
- **VAT registration threshold remains LKR 60m per 12 months.** The proposed reduction to
  LKR 36m was dropped before enactment of the VAT (Amendment) Act No. 14 of 2026, despite
  being widely reported as effective. Flagged prominently in the VAT reference.
- **Betting/gaming/liquor/tobacco corporate rate is 45%, not 40%** — raised by the Inland
  Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 2 of 2025 w.e.f. 1 April 2025.
